# First 100 Days: Shadow & Coach

A reusable Magazine block (type `shadowcoach`, labelled **First 100 Days: Shadow & Coach**), a demo article, and a role-restricted **First 100 Days** Work Tool that is a shell over demo data. The block is a manager's calibration: a Care Partner business owner reviews a fictional new coordinator's work, protects the participant first, tells a skill gap from a workflow defect, coaches without humiliation, assigns a two-week plan from content the Hub already has, and checks a second, different sample before a scoped readiness decision. The same unnamed coordinator recurs across the 100 days, so a later episode carries the history of an earlier one.

**Product principle:** the manager isn't taking the new hire's quiz. Completion is never evidence; an observed second sample is. A good coaching tone never cancels a missed safeguarding action, and a simulation score is never an HR record.

Source: _Partner Hub: First 100 Days — Shadow & Coach, PRD for Claude_ (21 November 2026). This is the PRD's phase 1 vertical slice (section 17, steps 1 to 5) with its first two scenarios, the author-side checks of phase 2 (step 6) in the editor, and the phase 2 workbench (step 7) as a **demo-data shell only**. The acceptance criteria (section 18) are tests.

In the Magazine editor: **Add a block → Simulate → First 100 Days: Shadow & Coach**. The Work Tool is at `/tools/first-100-days`.

> **Nothing here reads real employee or participant records.** Until Trilogy Care approves the data, consent, access and retention design, the workbench reads `content/shadowCoachDemo.ts` and its own tables only, and says so on the page. It holds no free text, no score and no ranking, so it can't become a performance-management or disciplinary record.

## The steps (PRD section 6)

1. **Brief**: who she is, what was handled, what you can see and what's unknown; an earlier episode's decision shows as history.
2. **Inspect and classify**: every line is a button that opens a labelled form: the kind of evidence (observed fact, reported allegation, assumption, missing information), what it needs (act now, clarify and correct, coach, process problem, no issue on current evidence), skill gap or workflow defect, and a one-sentence reason. "Something missing after this line?" marks an omission. No count of mistakes is shown.
3. **First actions**: each option is "Now, today", "After that" or not chosen. If an essential "now" action is missing, or an option raises a critical code, the step shows the correction and the missing route straight away (criterion 2), and **coaching can't start** until it's fixed. The first try is kept for the debrief.
4. **Coach**: up to three turns in the manager's own words, read for Notice, Ask, Agree and Verify, and for blame, humiliation, an HR label, a conclusion about the person or vagueness. The coordinator answers from authored replies (defensive, unsure, explains, agrees). Once she explains, she raises the source problem (the June sheet, or the fall-first form) and the manager chooses a response. "Pause and involve a reviewer" records a referral. Formative mode shows practice cues; assessed mode doesn't.
5. **Two-week plan**: proposed from authored suggestions by rule (no AI), each with its kind, the behaviour it practises, minutes, owner and due day. Each item that points at content shows what the server found: current (with its version), or not approved or not in the Hub, which **becomes an author review request** and never counts as learning (criterion 7). It needs one or two current learning pieces, one supervised practice and one observation; the immediate work action stays due today and is never a completion.
6. **Second sample**: a different artefact testing the same judgement. Each piece of evidence is marked with the Vetting Card's states. **No decision until every piece is marked** (criterion 8).
7. **Readiness decision**: sign off a defined task, continue supported practice, escalate to a reviewer, or fix the workflow. A sign-off is refused unless the manager has marked its evidence verified and named the reviewer the task needs. "Fully compliant coordinator" is offered, and is the critical out-of-scope error.
8. **Debrief**: critical errors first, the six dimensions in words with what was done and what was still to do, what the sample held, first actions (first try and final), the conversation with targeted feedback, a reviewed model and alternatives, the plan with versions and review requests, the second sample against the key, the coaching card with Save to Favourites, sources and versions, **Try again**.

## Scoring (`shadow-rubric-1`)

Six dimensions, 0 to 3 (PRD section 9): **evidence recognition**, **urgency and first action**, **role and source accuracy**, **coaching quality**, **learning selection**, **follow-up judgement**, capped by codes. **Critical errors** (no pass in assessed mode, whatever the total): a possible incident deferred to training or later; an allegation suppressed because the person or family asked; unverified contribution advice treated as a universal rate; advice that changed the participant's decision not corrected first; a real record changed from the training screen; a SIRS priority or reportability decided from incomplete facts; an unsupported clinical conclusion; a sign-off outside a defined task. Other codes lower a dimension: an urgent concern called something that can wait, coaching put before protection, a misread cause, an over-called accurate line, a promise, blame, humiliation, an HR label, a conclusion about the person, vagueness, no verification, a dismissed explanation, a reading-only plan, a repeated course, overload, no process issue, a sign-off on evidence the sample doesn't show.

**Completion** needs the sample inspected, first actions settled with nothing critical left out, a conversation, a plan shared, the second sample reviewed, a decision and the debrief on screen.

## The workbench (staged, demo data only)

`/tools/first-100-days`, for the business owner and the people they authorise (`plannerAccess`); anyone else, coordinators, Trilogy staff and authors included, gets "this workbench is for the business owner" and nothing more. Its Work Tools card shows only to them.

- **Two fictional, unnamed demo hires**, listed by start date, never by any measure. Each shows its stage (Days 0–14 to 61–100, adjustable), the stage's **evidence and outstanding checks** (never a progress bar), approved learning, observations against defined tasks, process issues, reviewer requests, and read-only QA or incident references ("not connected").
- **Actions**: assign existing learning (only content the server finds current and approved; anything else says it needs an author review), record an observation (defined task, outcome, date, shared or not; a sign-off that needs a reviewer needs that reviewer requested first), raise a process issue, request a reviewer, practise coaching (opens the published fictional practice), and **What she sees**: the coordinator's projection, with her plan and shared feedback only.
- **No free text, no score, no ranking.** Unknown fields (a score, a rank, a note) are dropped on save. Saves are versioned per business and idempotent by key; another business never sees them.
- **Data sources**: each real source a pilot would need is listed as not connected. **Who decides what**: the PRD's role table as a draft matrix, marked unapproved.

## Authoring, review and publishing

- **Editor**: plain fields for the frame, a JSON edit for the episodes, the live **case check**, and a **reviewer view** per episode: every finding and first action with its verdict, code and source, and every plan suggestion with the content it points at. The Learning Designer's selection rationale (signal, behaviour, barrier, why, alternatives, decision, measure). A disabled **AI** note: no AI coaching or drafting in this release. **Preview** plays any episode locally (every content item counts as available there; the server checks for real).
- **The case check** (structural; a demo sign-off doesn't cover it): unique line ids; findings point at real lines; an urgent finding has an essential "now" action; unsafe actions carry a code; a good pushback response exists; the plan has learning, practice and observation suggestions, and learning points at content; **the second sample repeats no line of the first** and comes later; a defined task exists; a defensible outcome exists; contribution claims match `RULESET` on the scenario's date; sources resolve; no identifier, no ranking words, and the coordinator is a role.
- **Sign-off** (inside the demo sign-off wrapper; "Publish anyway" can't waive it): the eight reviewers (owner, Care Coordination operations, Quality/incident, clinical governance, Support at Home policy/contributions, Partnerships, People/HR, privacy/security), the date, a review date not passed, the scenario version reviewed, the Job Aid, a pass mark if assessed, a rationale not rejected, and every cited source current and real (four Trilogy Care procedures are placeholders). The demo ships unsigned.
- **A source or rule change**: set a source to `review-required` (or let its review date pass) and publishing is held; `withdrawn` stops new attempts (410); a new contribution rule set fails the case check and pauses new attempts until the case is reassessed. Old attempts keep their frozen case and debrief.
- **Readers' copy**: the frame only. The view sends the sample, options (labels only), the plan with availability, and the second sample when its turn comes; never verdicts, codes, the key or the model wording.
- **Totals for authors** (`?stats=1`): attempts, people, submitted, completed, retries, versions; with five or more people, levels, outcomes, codes and critical errors; and the open author review requests.
- **Decision register**: the Problem desk export's **Shadow and Coach** sheet, one row per published episode, labelled simulation practice and kept apart from observations, QA, incident and HR records, with the content awaiting author review.

## Accessibility

Every decision is a labelled field or a button with words; marking a line is a button that opens a form, never a drag or a mouse-only highlight. On a phone, one step at a time with **View source** and **Your marks** a tap away; on a wide screen, source, step and evidence side by side. Focus moves to each step's heading when the step changes; saves are announced politely. Statuses and marks are words, never colour alone. No timer, motion or sound. Controls are at least 44 pixels tall.
