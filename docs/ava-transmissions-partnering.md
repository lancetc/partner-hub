# Ava's transmissions: Partnering with Trilogy Care (learning path)

A script for the ElevenLabs video (the Laura avatar, in Ava's voice) that introduces the learning path **Partnering with
Trilogy Care**, at the top of its page (`/paths/lp-partnering-with-trilogy-care`). Upload it in Admin → AI Learning
Guide → Ava's talking videos, against the path's line, with this script as what the video says (or press Fill in from
the video once it's uploaded).

Same Ava as the induction (docs/ava-transmissions-contractor-induction.md): a fictional regional care coordinator, warm
and dryly funny, out on the road, transmitting from the relay in her ute. She talks to partners as fellow
practitioners, never speaks as a real Trilogy Care employee ("Trilogy Care's clients", not "our clients"), and nothing
she says is a rule: the topics have the guidance.

The facts come from the path itself: for service partners and anyone in their team who refers clients, signs
agreements or sends invoices; eight topics in four chapters (Clients and Referrals, Getting Started, Getting Paid,
Growing Together), about an hour in all (62 minutes), in any order, with a certificate of completion. If the path
changes, change the script.

**Delivery.** Natural, a little breathless, on the move. `[static]` marks a crackle at the start; leave it out if the
tool reads it aloud. About 45 seconds.

---

Ava's introduction without the transmission: about 25 seconds spoken, or as written text. Use it for a simpler video,
or as the script for one.

> end.

## Topic hooks

### How Clients Get Support at Home Funding: the hook (first card)

Ava opens the Topic on its first card, the presenter card. It sets up the Topic's first decision (Mrs Novak and the
clean before her assessment) without answering it, then names what's coming: applying through My Aged Care, the
assessment, the two letters, and funding that turns out to be CHSP. No `[static]` marker, so it reads cleanly if Ava
reads it aloud over her portrait; add one at the start for a video if you like. About 30 seconds.

Paste it into the card's **Presenter script**, and into **Ava's script for this video** if you upload her video.

> It's Ava. Quick one before you start. A client's daughter rings you. Mum's assessment is on Thursday. Could your
> cleaner come on Wednesday and give the place a proper going-over, so it looks nice? Sounds kind, doesn't it? Hold
> that thought. Clients ask you how to get funded help all the time, and the answer always starts in the same place.
> Over the next few minutes, you'll follow the whole road: applying, the assessment, the two letters that come after,
> and how to spot funding that isn't Support at Home at all. Let's go.
