# Activity Catalogue: audit, AI selection and homepage showcase

Built from the brief *Activity Catalogue, AI selection and homepage showcase* (26 November 2026). This note records what the scan of the code and the dev database actually found, what was changed, how AI selection now works for Magazine, and what is left for Trilogy Care to decide. The code is the source of truth; anything the scan couldn't establish is marked **unknown**.

- Authors: **Administration → Content → Activity Catalogue** (`/admin/activities`). Live totals, search and filters, a detail view per activity, the **Needs attention** filter, the homepage picks and an Excel export of exactly what's on screen (`/api/admin/activities/export`).
- Learners: **Practise the decisions you make every day** on the homepage, under the workspace, and **Explore all interactive activities** (`/activities`).
- Authors drafting a Magazine with AI: each outline page now carries **Recommended activities** with **Why this block?**; generated blocks carry it too.

### What the scan found that matters

1. **The Magazine AI builder used a hand-written block list.** `BLOCK_SHAPES` in `magazineDraft.ts` named 26 simple blocks and nothing else, and `pageIssues` banned quiz, video, spot, carousel and hotspots by hand. None of the 34 specialist simulators, roleplays and calculators could be recommended, and nothing explained why a block was chosen. **Fixed:** the shapes are now a per-type map the catalogue gates (`AI_SHAPES`, `blockShapes()`), the bans come from the catalogue, and the outline asks for activities from a catalogue shortlist with reasons (below).
2. **Almost every published specialist course is a demo edition.** Of 26 published courses, 24 were published under a demo sign-off (`magazine_demo_editions`): no named reviewer approved them. Only *The client journey* and *Maya’s next coordinator: when to hire* aren't demo editions. On this dev Hub (demo sign-off on) the showcase may feature demo editions; on a Hub with demo sign-off off, they are left out automatically, and only the hiring lab would qualify for a card.
3. **A test course is published to everyone:** `zz-test-hiring-runway-window` (audience: everyone). It makes the Where Is the Line? block look published. It's excluded from the showcase, the directory and the AI's reuse list, and flagged in Admin. Not archived: that's the owner's call.
4. **20 registered blocks are in no course or draft:** guide, ava (new today), carousel, hotspots, spot, journey, reset, myths, redpen, signal, mission, balance, bridge, baton, budget, sandbox, branching, reflection, benchmark, mentor. They render and author (checked by the test) but have no instance to open, so their **Renderable** is **unknown** and none is promotable.
5. **Standalone tools were easy to mistake for blocks.** The Caseload-to-Margin calculator, Network Builder, Referral Studio, Trust Check and First 100 Days Work Tools are private, planner-gated pages, not embeddable; each now has its own entry marked *Unavailable to the AI builder* with the matching block named (hiring, network, referralstudio, trustcheck, shadowcoach).
6. **The three Pocket Playbook tools are pilot drafts** (Contribution Quick-Calc, Incident first steps, Golden Scripts): open to their roles, not approved by their owner, so not promotable. **While You Wait (live call)** isn't released, so it shows as not renderable.
7. **The Topic builder doesn't select activities.** It always writes the same seven-stage pattern. Its card types are catalogued with that reason (AI-ready: partly).
8. **Field names clash on blocks.** The Job Aid block already has a `why` and the notes deck a `selection`, so the AI's reasons are stored on a block as `aiWhy` (Claude still writes `"why"`, and the server moves it).

Names from the design work, and where they landed (aliases resolve to one entry; saved content is unchanged): Quarterly Budget Decision Lab → `budgetlab`; 72-Hour Discharge Pivot → `discharge`; Excluded Item / Invoice X-Ray → `xray`; Tough Family Call → `phonecall`; Contractor Compliance → `auditor` (Contractor Vetting Card); Journey Map → `onedaymap` (multi-persona) and `journey` (SaH Journey); Hiring Runway → `hiring` (and the `tool.caseload-margin` Work Tool); Next 10 Clients → `intake`; Coordinator Week → `workload`; Suburb Growth Readiness → `coverage`; Referral Journey → `referral`; Missed Service Cost → `reliability`; 90-Day Cash Bridge → `cashbridge`; Job Aids → `playbook` and `tool.job-aids`; SIRS Red-Flag → `tool.incident-first-steps` (and `signal` for reportable-incident cases); hazard scenarios → `hazardscan`; scope of practice → `line`; Ava-hosted story activities → `magazine.ava-relay` (page feature) and the new `ava` block.

## AI selection for Magazine: how it works now

1. **Understand the problem.** The outline prompt asks Claude to name the behaviour and the evidence, and to say in `questions` when a complaint looks like a process or product problem rather than a training gap.
2. **Search for reuse.** `reuseCandidates` offers up to five published courses (never test courses) whose title and standfirst share words with the brief. Claude may answer with `reuse`; a slug it wasn't offered is refused. Accepted reuse suggestions reach the author as questions (“may already cover this… link or adapt it?”).
3. **Query eligible candidates.** `magazineCandidates` (lib) returns every block the catalogue marks *AI configurable*, plus up to eight *AI recommended* specialist blocks that best match the brief (by the registry's own text match and aliases), filtered by the course audience (owner-only labs are only offered to a course partners can read). Human-only and unavailable activities are never offered. Each candidate goes into the prompt as one line: id, name, “you write it” or “specialist setup: recommend only”, time, purpose, good for, poor fit, rules.
4. **Recommend a sequence.** Each outline page may have up to four `activities`, each with `why`: behaviour, evidence, alternative considered, rationale, learner action, measured signal.
5. **Validate deterministically.** `outlineIssues` refuses an id not offered or a `why` missing its parts (Claude is asked again); `cleanActivities` drops anything the catalogue doesn't allow, and takes the **mode from the catalogue, never the model**, so a specialist simulator can't be passed off as configurable. The review owner defaults to the block's sign-off and the source to the page's documents. At page generation, `pageIssues` refuses any block the catalogue doesn't let Claude configure, or that wasn't offered for the course, and a `budget` block that isn't flagged illustrative.
6. **Human review.** In **Draft with AI → 3. Review the outline**, each page shows **Recommended activities**: accept, reject, reorder, remove, swap for any eligible activity, and edit every part of **Why this block?**. A rejected activity is never written. Decisions are audited (`activity.recommendation_accepted`, `_rejected`, `_edited`), as are `activity.recommended` and `activity.added_to_draft`, with the catalogue version.
7. **On the page.** Generated blocks carry `aiWhy`; the editor shows a **Why this block?** badge and an editable panel under *For reviewers* (learners never see it). A specialist activity the outline kept is listed after generation as **Specialist setup still to do on this page**, with its sign-off: the author adds it from *Add block* and sets it up from its reviewed case. The AI never writes its rules, figures, thresholds or answer key.
8. **After publication.** Learner use of each block is in `magazine_events` and the block's attempts table; the Admin catalogue's *Selection and showcase* table shows, over 90 days, recommended / accepted (of recommended) / rejected / swapped / added to a draft / shown on home / opened. It says what was chosen and opened, never that work changed.

Before: 26 blocks could be written by the AI, 0 specialist activities could be recommended, and no choice carried a reason. After: 27 configurable (the Hub diagram added), 34 recommendable with specialist setup, every recommendation and generated block with *Why this block?*. Revise with AI (`magazineRevise.ts`) uses the same catalogue-derived shapes and page check.

**Other builders:** not changed. The Topic builder's fixed pattern isn't a selection step, and Topics can't hold Magazine blocks; Challenges and Discovery Paths reuse Topics and courses. Adapting the catalogue query to Topics needs the Topic builder to choose card types first (see blockers).

## Homepage and directory

- **Practise the decisions you make every day** sits under the welcome and assigned work. It shows the first four editorial picks the person can open, all at once (a row on wide screens, a stack on phones, no carousel), each with its question, the course cover, kind and time, audience, a fictional-practice label and its action (**Resume** where the course has their place). Then one **Use it at work** tile, visibly apart: the Caseload-to-Margin calculator for people with planner access, otherwise a current approved Job Aid, otherwise nothing. The whole section disappears when nothing qualifies.
- Picks start from `DEFAULT_SHOWCASE` (eight real published courses) and are chosen, ordered, scheduled (from/to) and unfeatured in Admin; saving stores them in `settings` (`activity_showcase`) and is audited. A pick disappears by itself when its course is archived, unpublished, outside the person's audience, a demo edition on a Hub without demo sign-off, a test course, or no longer holds the activity.
- Checked on this dev Hub: a partner (sam) sees hiring, hazard, cash and trust; a coordinator (riley) sees discharge, family call, quote and hazard; a business owner (casey) sees the partner set plus the calculator tile; an author sees the coordinator set with the hiring lab as **Resume**.
- `/activities` lists every published course built around a practice activity and every tool or game the person's role and access allow, filtered by role, kind and intent. Withdrawn, unreleased and planner-only tools aren't shown to people who can't open them.
- Events (`activity_events`): `shown_on_homepage` and `opened`, with the activity id and version, the surface, the course slug and revision, and the person's audience. Never answers, notes or business figures.
