# Ava's transmissions: Ready for Your First Shift (Contractor Induction)

Scripts for the ElevenLabs videos (the Laura avatar, in Ava's voice). One per Ava line in the course: the cover, then
pages 1 to 8. Upload each in Admin → AI Learning Guide → Ava's talking videos, against its line, and paste the script
into "What this video says" so Read script matches what learners hear.

## The thread

Ava is doing the western run: out of Toowoomba, through Dalby, Miles and Roma to Charleville and beyond, meeting new
contractors and checking in on homes along the way. She transmits from the small mobile relay in her ute, so the signal
comes and goes, and most transmissions are cut off. The learner travels the induction alongside her, a page per stop;
on the last page the signal finally comes through clear.

From Ava's established story (the Character Library): she's a fictional regional care coordinator, warm and dryly
funny, who talks to the learner as a fellow practitioner; she keeps a scuffed notebook headed "What did I miss?"; early
in her career she arranged book deliveries for Mrs Bell, who went to the library for her Thursday friends as much as for
the books. She never speaks as a real Trilogy Care employee, and nothing she says is a rule: each transmission points
to the page, which has the guidance.

**Delivery.** Natural and a little breathless, like someone talking on the move. Where a script ends on a dash, the
transmission cuts there: stop mid-word, no wind-down. `[static]` marks a crackle or a moment of dropout; leave it out
if the tool reads it aloud. Each one runs about 30 to 40 seconds.
